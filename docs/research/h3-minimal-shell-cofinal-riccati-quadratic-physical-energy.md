# Cofinal minimal-shell Riccati windows require quadratic physical H³ energy

Base: `862a31e4` (accepted every-tail signed replenishment or recurrent Riccati windows).

On any genuinely selected physical Riccati-heavy time `s` from a minimal
first-crossing window, put `R = v(k)+1`, `E = velocityH3EnergyAt u s`, and
`K = h3PathSqrtEnergyRiccatiCoefficient` (nonnegative, constant).
The existing Riccati alternative reads

    R³/2 < K sqrt(E) E.

Squaring with both factors nonnegative and using `sqrt(E)² = E` gives

    R⁶ < 4 K² E³.

The new scalar Lean lemma proves the explicit **quadratic amplitude cost**

    R² < (4 K² + 1) E.

The +1 keeps the multiplier strictly positive and avoids division by K.

A new physical theorem lifts this to the original selected window's actual
physical H³ energy. The cofinal theorem preserves the *same* `t,N,m,C,δ,v,j`
and shift `k₁`, minimal shell energy windows, source sixth/ninth rate
alternative, positive raw Fourier shell, normalized energy-width barrier,
strictly disjoint PDE clocks and every-tail signed-gap accounting.

Under BOTH explicit assumptions:

1. The certified cubic window charges are unbounded on every late tail.
2. The positive integrated signed-gap transport partial sums are bounded on
   each late tail.

it forces arbitrarily late selected windows with a physical time `s` satisfying

    (v(k)+1)² < (4 K² + 1) velocityH3EnergyAt u s.

This strengthens recurrence of an abstract Riccati exception to a concrete
quadratic physical H³ energy lower bound. It does not establish either
conditional budget, global H³ regularity, or an unconditional contradiction.
