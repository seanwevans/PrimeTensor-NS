# High-energy corridor synchronized with radial concentration (checkpoint 15e85bf7)

The four fixed channels on the no-physical-L²-trace branch are not generally
simultaneous. Two cofinal subsets of a half-open time interval need not
intersect. The high-H³-energy channel, however, yields radial concentration
at its OWN selected times, because the old physical kinetic energy bounds
low-radial H³ Fourier mass after any fixed positive-time anchor.

For R=n+1 and K(R)=1+R²+R⁴+R⁶, let C_R=K(R)E₀(b) with b=(a+T)/2.
Let A=48 C1>0. We request from the cofinal high-energy corridor:

    A*((R+2)*(C_R+2)+1) <= A*sqrt(E_H3(t)).

This guarantees E_H3(t) > 1+(R+1)*(C_R+1).
The exact Fourier energy identity, together with LowMass_R<=C_R, implies

    R*(C_R+1) < HighMass_R,
    (R+1)*HighMass_R > R*(E_H3(t)-1).

Exact physical spectral dissipation then yields

    R^3 * E_H3(t) < (R+2)*D_H3(t).

Select t_n within distance 1/R_n of T after requesting the threshold.
For n>=1, the physical D_H3(t_n)/E_H3(t_n) ratio exceeds R_n and tends
to infinity. These are one-sequence, same-time inequalities, unlike the
separately obtained no-trace radial witness and fixed signed-channel witness.
