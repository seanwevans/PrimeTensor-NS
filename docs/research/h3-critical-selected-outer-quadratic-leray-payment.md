# Selected outer carrier: full quadratic Leray payment\n\nBase: `3277b04bc`.\n\n## Exact physical ledger\n\nWith \(H=P_{\ge\rho}u(x)\), \(A=P_{\ge\rho}\int_x^s\Delta u(t)\,dt\), \(B=P_{\ge\rho}\int_x^s\mathcal N(u(t))\,dt\), \(D=P_{\ge\rho}\Delta u(x)\), \(C=A-(s-x)D\), the existing proved physical Bochner FTC yields\n\n\[M_x-M_s=2(s-x)d_\rho(x)-2\langle H,C\rangle-\|A\|^2+2\langle H+A,B\rangle-\|B\|^2,\qquad d_\rho(x)=-\langle H,D\rangle\ge0.\]\n\n## Improvement\n\nIf the selected deficit forces \(\epsilon<K(M_x-M_s)\), then exact algebra and Cauchy--Schwarz give\n\n\[\epsilon-K(2(s-x)d_\rho+2\|H\|\|C\|)+K(\|A\|^2+\|B\|^2)<2K\langle H+A,B\rangle.\]\n\nIf the first two channels do not cover epsilon, the nonlinear Leray correction must pay **both** squared increments. In particular, \(\|A\|^2<2\|H+A\|\|B\|\) and \(\|A\|^2<\|H+A\|^2\). Thus there is a genuine minimum action and nontrivial geometry for Leray transfer. The exact physical Fourier sign identifies \(d_\rho\ge0\) without additional assumptions.\n\n## Temporal commutator\n\nA cutoff-independent but coarse bound follows from the highpass contraction:\n\n\[\|C\|\le\left\|\int_x^s\Delta u(t)\,dt\right\|+(s-x)\|\Delta u(x)\|.\]\n\nThis does **not** prove a terminal-uniform modulus or any decay of the frequency-amplified source product. It is not an H3 continuation theorem.
