# Six-sector integral Hermitian pairing for the full physical H³ commutator

Checkpoint: `85562c24`, the proved phase-free imaginary kernel identity.

The physical real divergence-free Schwartz velocity's complete full-third
commutator is `(2*pi)^7 * integral (Im K)`. Previous work established
pointwise Hermitian evenness `Im K(-p,-r) = Im K(p,r)`.

This module establishes that simultaneous negation of both Fourier variables
preserves `volume.prod volume` using the genuine product measure-preserving
map. Every pair of measurable reflection-matched regions has identical
`Im K` integrals, in particular open or closed momentum-pair balls about
opposite centers. Six explicitly indexed positive coherent pairs yield an
exact equality of labelled local sums:

    twelve_paired_ball_sum = 2 * six_positive_ball_sum.

This identity does not imply the global integral equals the twelve-ball
sum: finite balls may overlap and a general tube need not equal their
union. To obtain an actual whole-space sector identity, the next geometry
proof must establish disjointness and exact covering of the kernel's support
(at an appropriate packet width). Only after this is it meaningful to derive
a global sign from the six positive orientation contributions. Neither
that partition nor a strict sign is assumed or asserted here.
