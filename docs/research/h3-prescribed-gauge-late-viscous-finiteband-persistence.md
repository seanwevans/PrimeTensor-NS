**Base commit:** `b5d2121544c43d13d0c959182f87868fa3ac22c0` — actual late H³ viscous spikes from prescribed-gauge source escape.

## Mathematically new bridge

The previously proved cofinal prescribed-gauge four-channel theorem yields, on its last arm, an actual time `r ∈ (m,T)` with

`V = H(v k)^4 < P D(r)`,

where `D(r) = velocityH3DissipationAt u r`, `m=(τ(v k)+T)/2`, `Q=N(v k)+2`, and

`P = 168 * Q^4 * ‖Fourier‖ * (3 + C_L) * (T-m)`.

Since `V>0` and `D(r)≥0`, **P must be strictly positive**; no independent Fourier-isometry norm hypothesis is needed. Hence `V/P < D(r)`. The repository already proves `h3Critical_fullViscousSpike_has_finiteBandIntegratedRightWindow`: this strict full-H³ spike admits a finite radial cutoff J and a positive right-hand interval `[r,r+η]` with `r+η<T` on which

`V/P < D_low,J(s) ≤ D(s)`.

This is legitimate because **finite low-radial dissipation is continuous in physical time**. It does not assume continuity of the unbounded full H³ dissipation.

Because full H³ dissipation is interval-integrable, pointwise integration and monotonicity yield the quantitative tail charge

`η * (V/P) ≤ ∫_r^(r+η) D(s) ds ≤ ∫_m^T D(s) ds`.

The rightmost integral tends to zero on the inherited cofinal minimal-crossing witnesses. Thus any late spike generated by the fourth branch must pay a **terminally vanishing real viscous budget** for its finite-band persistence window. This is not an upper bound on J or a lower bound on η.

## Remaining hard PDE problem

The captured cutoff `J` and persistence width `η` depend on the actual late spike. They may be very large and extremely small, respectively. The theorem makes the missing lower bound on `η` relative to its own frequency and physical signed-transport coefficient explicit. These new persistence windows are *not* asserted disjoint from one another and are not the original minimal-attained source clocks. Neither unconditional H³ continuation nor a contradiction has been proved.
