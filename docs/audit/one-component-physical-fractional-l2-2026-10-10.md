# Physical L² fractional realization after `0b8e42e1`

This module uses Mathlib's inverse `L²` Fourier isometry to realize the
already-proved Ḣ^(3/2) multiplier as a physical-space complex `L²` object.

The physical norm squared equals the exact Fourier energy of the actual
logged velocity component, and both transverse fractional physical `L²`
norms are `L²`-integrable in time on one common terminal interval under the
existing physical-curl endpoint and energy-class hypotheses.

The construction does not yet prove real-valuedness of the fractional
derivative or equivalence with an independently specified distributional
Sobolev derivative. In particular it does not prove or postulate the published
Liu–Zhang continuation theorem, the paper's strong-solution eligibility
conditions, or unconditional Navier–Stokes regularity.
